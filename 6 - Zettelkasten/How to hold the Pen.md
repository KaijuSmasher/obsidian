# How to hold the Pen
There are two kinds of grips which are commonly used:
## Overhand Grip
The first one is the overhand grip which is optimal in allowing us to use all sides of, for example, a charcoal/led pencil enabling us to use them to make a large variety of strokes. It's also pretty good to force us into using the shoulder since the wrist is more locked up while holding a pencil this way.
![[Overhand Grip.png]]

## Tripod Grip
The other one is the tripod grip, which is used for tools like mechanical pencils that only have the tip to use, so only one dimension of variability. It's the way we are used to holding pencils, because it's the grip we are used to for writing.
